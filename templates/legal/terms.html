Terms of Use

Last updated 28 September 2026 · by creating an account you agree to these terms.

What FoodBridge is

FoodBridge connects food donors, community pantries and volunteer drivers: a listing is offered to the nearest pantry, then the nearest driver, with a timer at each step so nothing sits waiting. We coordinate the match: we don't prepare, store, inspect or transport food ourselves.

Accounts & approval
  • Every new Donor, Recipient and Driver account is reviewed by an admin before it can use the portal.
  • Give accurate details and keep your login private: you're responsible for activity on your account.
  • Admins may change a role, or suspend or deactivate an account that breaks these terms.
Food safety
  • Donors list only food that's safe to eat, correctly stored and within its use-by or best-before date, described honestly: including storage needs and known allergens.
  • Pantries and drivers may refuse food that looks unsafe or doesn't match its listing, and should report it through the feedback form so an admin can follow up.
  • Follow the food-safety rules that apply where you live. FoodBridge doesn't certify any food or any partner.
Offers, claims & cancellations
  • Offers are time-limited: don't respond in time and it moves to the next nearest partner.
  • Only claim or accept what you can genuinely collect or deliver, and give a real reason when you cancel, release or withdraw.
  • Drivers need an accurate area and street address on their profile before they can accept a pickup.
Acceptable use

No false listings, harassment, spam, or attempts to access another person's account or data.

No guarantees

FoodBridge is provided as-is by a student team. We can't guarantee a listing will be claimed, collected or delivered, or that the service is always available. Distance, meal-count and CO₂ figures shown in reports are estimates, not measurements.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continuing to use FoodBridge after a change means you accept it.
